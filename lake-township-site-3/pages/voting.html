Voting in Lake Township

Registering to vote, voting absentee, and keeping your records up to date.

How do I register to vote?

Michigan residents can register to vote online, by mail, or in person:

  • Online: Michigan Voter Information Center (michigan.gov/sos).
  • In Person: Visit the Township Clerk or Benzie County Clerk. Same-day registration is available at your polling location on Election Day.
  • Address changes: Update with the Township Clerk AND with the Michigan Secretary of State if you are also changing your driver’s license address.

How do I vote absentee?

All registered Michigan voters may vote absentee — no reason required.

  • Request a ballot online at the Michigan Voter Information Center or from the Township Clerk, or join the permanent mail ballot list to receive a ballot automatically for every election.
  • Return your completed ballot to the Township Clerk by 8:00 PM on Election Day — if it’s close to the election, deliver it in person rather than mailing.

How do I change my address for voting and tax records?

Notify all of the following Township officials so your records are updated correctly:

  • Township Clerk — voting registration records
  • Township Treasurer — tax bill mailing address
  • Township Assessor — property assessment records

Note: a Township address change does not update your Michigan driver’s license or state voter registration — update those separately at a Michigan Secretary of State office or at michigan.gov/sos.

Voter Registration Form

Form ED-109W (Michigan Voter Registration Application) is available directly from the Michigan Secretary of State:

  • Michigan Voter Information Center — register, check status, or request an absentee ballot

Election Day

Ballots must be returned to the Township Clerk by 8:00 PM on Election Day.
