Supervisor

Township leadership and board administration.

Mary J. Pitcher — supervisor@laketwp.org

The Supervisor provides leadership to the Township Board, staff, and residents, and serves as the presiding and executive officer of the Township Board. The Supervisor’s duties include managing Township operations and being accountable to the Board of Trustees and the public for the efficient administration of Township government. The Supervisor serves as Secretary of the Board of Review and acts as the Township’s legal agent for the transaction of legal business, works with fellow board members to develop the Township budget, and appoints members to the Planning Commission and other committees and commissions, subject to approval by a majority vote of the Township Board. The Supervisor may also assume the responsibilities of the personnel director for Township employees when so delegated by the Township Board.

Statutory Duties

  • Presides over and moderates Township Board meetings; regulates proceedings, decides questions of order, and declares votes
  • Secretary to the Board of Review
  • Acts as the Township’s legal agent
  • Maintains records of the Supervisor’s office
  • Develops the budget in conjunction with fellow Board members
  • Appoints Committee and Commission members
  • Member of the Elections Commission
  • May call special meetings
  • May appoint a deputy
  • May administer the oath of office
