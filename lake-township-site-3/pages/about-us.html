About Lake Township

Local government for a nine-lake, Lake Michigan shoreline community in Benzie County.

Township Board

Lake Township is governed by an elected Township Board, led by the Township Supervisor and supported by the Clerk, Treasurer, and Trustees. For individual contact information, see the Staff Directory or the Supervisor page.

Office Hours

The Township office is at 5153 N Scenic Hwy, Honor, MI 49640.

  • Monday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Wednesday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Friday: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Hours may vary on holidays — call ahead to confirm at 231-325-5202.

Meetings

Township Board meetings are held on the first Thursday of each month at 7:00 PM at Township Hall (5153 N Scenic Hwy, Honor, MI 49640). All meetings are open to the public and include a public comment period. Special meetings (Board of Review, Planning Commission, joint hearings) are also posted on the Township Hall bulletin board.

For a full schedule, posted agendas, and meeting minutes, see Meetings & Agendas.
