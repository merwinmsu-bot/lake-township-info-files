Category 1: Permits, Licenses & Applications

How do I apply for a Land Use (Zoning) Permit?

A Land Use Permit is required before constructing, placing, or expanding any structure over 100 square feet, or changing the use of any land or building in Lake Township. To apply:

  • Download the Land Use Permit Application and review the Application Instructions. Note: three completed copies are required.
  • Email the completed application to zoning_admin@laketwp.org, or bring it to the Township office.
  • See the Contact Us page for mailing address and phone number.

How do I apply for a Short-Term Rental (STR) license?

Lake Township requires a license for all short-term rentals (rentals of fewer than 30 consecutive days) under STR Ordinance 2023-001. Requirements include:

  • Designating a 24/7 local contact person.
  • Passing a safety inspection (smoke/CO detectors, fire extinguishers).
  • Download the STR Application and the Guide for Short-Term Renters from the Documents & Forms page.
  • Submit the completed application to the Township office or Zoning Administrator.

How do I apply for a ZBA variance?

If you need relief from a specific zoning requirement (setback, lot size, height, etc.), you may apply to the Zoning Board of Appeals for a variance.

  • Download the ZBA Application and the ZBA General Information sheet before filing.
  • Contact the Zoning Administrator to confirm the next ZBA meeting date and application deadline.

How do I apply for a parcel division (land split)?

To divide a parcel in Lake Township you must file a Parcel Division application.

  • Download the Parcel Division Application from the Documents & Forms page.
  • Contact the Zoning Administrator or Township office for guidance on the review process.

How do I volunteer or apply for a board or committee position?

Lake Township welcomes community members for several boards and committees:

  • Planning Commission
  • Zoning Board of Appeals
  • Board of Review
  • Election Inspectors
  • Park Attendants

Contact the Township Clerk to ask about current openings. See the Contact Us page for contact information.

Category 2: Taxes & Payments

How do I pay my property taxes?

Lake Township property taxes are collected twice per year. Payment options:

  • Online: BS&A Online payment portal — search by name, address, or parcel number.
  • Drop Box: A secure drop box is available at the Township office for after-hours payments.
  • In Person or by Mail: 5153 N Scenic Hwy, Honor, MI 49640.
  • Summer taxes are due September 14. Winter taxes are due February 14.
  • Office hours: Monday and Wednesday 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM; Friday 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM.

How do I pay my property taxes after the deadline?

Summer taxes are mailed at the end of June (due September 14); winter taxes are mailed at the end of November (due February 14). After the deadline, penalties and interest accrue and payment must be made to the Benzie County Treasurer.

  • Online: Use the Benzie County delinquent tax portal. [PLACEHOLDER: Confirm direct URL with Benzie County Treasurer's office before publishing.]
  • By Mail or In Person: Contact the Benzie County Treasurer’s office for the address and hours.

How do I appeal my property assessment?

Assessment change notices are mailed in late February. If you believe your assessment is incorrect, you may appeal to the March Board of Review.

  • File Form 618 / L-4035: Petition to Board of Review and contact the Township Assessor before the March meeting — see the Contact Us page.
  • Board of Review dates are posted on the Township home page and hall bulletin board each year.
  • Decisions of the Board of Review may be further appealed to the Michigan Tax Tribunal by the state’s deadlines.

Category 3: Contact & Find Us

How do I contact Township staff?

Staff can be reached by phone, email, or in person. The Contact Us page lists the Supervisor, Clerk, Treasurer, Assessor, and Zoning Administrator with individual contact information.

What are the Township office hours and where is the office?

The Township office is at 5153 N Scenic Hwy, Honor, MI 49640.

  • Monday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Wednesday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Friday: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Get directions

Hours may vary on holidays — call ahead or check the Contact Us page to confirm.

How do I change my address for voting and tax records?

Notify all of the following Township officials so your records are updated correctly:

  • Township Clerk — voting registration records
  • Township Treasurer — tax bill mailing address
  • Township Assessor — property assessment records

See the Contact Us page for contact information. Note: a Township address change does not update your Michigan driver’s license or state voter registration — update those separately at a Michigan Secretary of State office or at michigan.gov/sos.

Where is Lake Township Park and how do I get a parking permit?

Lake Township Park is at the end of Lake Michigan Road where the Platte River meets Lake Michigan — a scenic, dog-friendly site and one of the few public Lake Michigan access points in the area.

  • Day parking fee: $5.00 — pay the lot attendant or use the payment tubes.
  • Season pass: $40.00.
  • Season passes are available at the Township office during regular office hours.

How do I report a road problem (potholes, plowing, downed trees)?

Roads in Lake Township are not maintained by the Township:

  • County roads (including Lake Michigan Road and most local roads): contact the Benzie County Road Commission.
  • M-22 (Scenic Highway) is a state highway — report problems through MDOT’s Report a Road Concern system at michigan.gov/mdot.
  • A tree or hazard blocking a road, or any dangerous situation: call 911.

How do I attend or participate in Township meetings?

Board meetings are held on the first Thursday of each month at 7:00 PM at Township Hall (5153 N Scenic Hwy, Honor, MI 49640). All meetings are open to the public and include a public comment period.

How do I schedule trash pickup?

Lake Township does not provide curbside trash collection. Residents arrange their own service directly with a private hauler — a few haulers currently operate in Benzie County; contact them for pricing and to confirm they serve your road.

  • Large items: The Township hosts free Clean-Up Days each year for residents to dispose of bulky items. The next Clean-Up Day is Saturday, August 8, 2026, 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM at the Lake Township Hall (M-22 & Lake Michigan Road, south driveway entrance). Not accepted: automotive batteries and tires, chemicals or hazardous waste, motor oil, paint and thinners, yard waste, concrete, refrigerators containing Freon, and lumber over 4 feet. No commercial or contractor dumping.
  • Recycling: Curbside recycling is not available. Benzie County operates free 24/7 recycling drop-off sites throughout the county — see the county’s Materials Management page or call the Benzie County Recycling Coordinator at 231-882-0554.
  • Questions? Contact the Township office at 231-325-5202.

How do I find recycling drop-off locations?

Lake Township participates in Benzie County’s recycling program. See the Benzie County Materials Management (Recycling) page for current drop-off locations, hours, and accepted materials.

When are Township Clean-Up Days?

Clean-Up Days are typically held on the second Saturdays of June and August, 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM (or until trucks are full). Dates vary each year — watch the Upcoming Events section on the Township home page for current-year details.

How do I register to vote?

Michigan residents can register to vote online, by mail, or in person:

  • Online: Michigan Voter Information Center (michigan.gov/sos).
  • In Person: Visit the Township Clerk or Benzie County Clerk. Same-day registration is available at your polling location on Election Day.
  • Address changes: Update with the Township Clerk AND with the Michigan Secretary of State if you are also changing your driver’s license address.

How do I vote absentee?

All registered Michigan voters may vote absentee — no reason required.

  • Request a ballot online at the Michigan Voter Information Center or from the Township Clerk, or join the permanent mail ballot list to receive a ballot automatically for every election.
  • Return your completed ballot to the Township Clerk by 8:00 PM on Election Day — if it’s close to the election, deliver it in person rather than mailing.

Category 5: Zoning, Codes & Ordinances

How do I find the Township zoning ordinance and local codes?

All Township ordinances — including the full Zoning Ordinance organized by article, the Short-Term Rental Ordinance, the Nuisance Ordinance, and others — are available on the Documents & Forms page.

  • For questions about what is permitted on a specific property, contact the Zoning Administrator at zoning_admin@laketwp.org.
  • The current Zoning Map is also available on the Documents & Forms page.

How do I find information about Short-Term Rental rules?

Short-term rentals (fewer than 30 consecutive days) require a Township license under STR Ordinance 2023-001, effective November 30, 2023. Key requirements:

  • Licensed STRs must have a 24/7 local contact person available.
  • Safety equipment (smoke/CO detectors, fire extinguishers) must be present and inspected.
  • Guests receive a Guide for Short-Term Renters with local rules and emergency contacts.
  • Download the STR Application from the Documents & Forms page.

How do I report a problem with a short-term rental?

Every licensed short-term rental must have a 24/7 local contact person who can respond to problems.

  • For noise, parking, or other issues at a rental, contact the property’s designated local contact first — renters and neighbors should have access to this information.
  • If the problem continues or the rental appears to be unlicensed, contact the Zoning Administrator at zoning_admin@laketwp.org with the property address and details.
  • For emergencies or disturbances in progress, call 911.

What can I build on my property — and do I need a permit?

Almost all construction in Lake Township requires a Land Use Permit first. Key rules:

  • All structures requires a Land Use Permit before construction begins.
  • Setbacks, height limits, and impervious surface coverage limits apply in all zoning districts.
  • Waterfront properties in the Lakeshore Residential (L/R) district have additional shoreline buffer and vegetation requirements.
  • If your lot is below the minimum size for your district, contact the Zoning Administrator — special nonconforming lot rules may apply.
  • For the full permit application and fee schedule, see the Documents & Forms page.

What is the proposed new Zoning Ordinance and how can I give input?

The Planning Commission is developing a comprehensive new Zoning Ordinance (Draft B1, October 2025) to replace the current ordinance. Key changes include:

  • Stronger environmental protections for Crystal Lake, including a mandatory 35-foot shoreline vegetation buffer and impervious surface caps.
  • New provisions for accessory dwelling units (ADUs), solar energy, and short-term rentals.
  • A reorganized, three-tier permit approval system (ZA / Site Plan Review / Special Use Permit).

What local ordinances should I know about?

Beyond zoning, Lake Township has adopted several regulatory ordinances, all available on the Documents & Forms page:

  • Fireworks Ordinance — when consumer fireworks may be used.
  • Off-Road Vehicles Ordinance — where and how ORVs may operate on township roads.
  • Junk Ordinance and Nuisance Ordinance — property maintenance and nuisance conditions.
  • Aquatic Nuisances Ordinance — preventing the spread of aquatic invasive species.
  • Truck Route Ordinance — designated routes for heavy trucks.
  • Prohibition of Recreational Marihuana Establishments — marijuana businesses are not permitted in the Township.
  • Floodplain Management Ordinance — requirements for building in flood-prone areas.

Category 7: Environment & Natural Resources

How do I get a burn permit?

A burn permit is required before any open burning of leaves, brush, or other vegetation. Get a free DNR burn permit at dnr.state.mi.us/burnpermits, and follow Township burning rules.

  • Burn only natural wood and vegetation — never trash, plastics, or treated lumber — and never burn on windy days.
  • Township Clean-Up Days and county recycling are better options for junk and large debris.

How do I get a septic or well permit?

Septic systems and wells are permitted by the Benzie-Leelanau District Health Department — contact them before installing, replacing, or repairing a system.

  • Have your septic tank inspected and pumped every 3–5 years — a failing system can pollute groundwater and nearby lakes.

Do I need a permit for shoreline or beach work?

Usually, yes. Work along Lake Michigan, Crystal Lake, Platte Lake, Little Platte Lake, or the Platte River is regulated to protect water quality and prevent erosion — ask before you disturb any ground near the water.

  • Seawalls, riprap, dredging, filling, and work below the ordinary high-water mark generally require a permit from EGLE; critical dune and high-risk erosion area rules may also apply on Lake Michigan.
  • Earth changes near lakes and streams require a Benzie County soil erosion and sedimentation control permit.
  • Contact the Zoning Administrator at zoning_admin@laketwp.org first — they can tell you which approvals your project needs.

How do I find out if my property is in the Crystal Lake Watershed Overlay District?

Properties that drain to Crystal Lake are subject to additional zoning standards for construction, landscaping, tree removal, steep slopes, and impervious surfaces under the Crystal Lake Watershed Overlay District.

  • Contact the Zoning Administrator at zoning_admin@laketwp.org to confirm whether the overlay applies to your parcel.
  • Background and ordinance information is available from the Crystal Lake Watershed Association.

How do I help stop invasive species?

Clean, drain, and dry every boat, trailer, and piece of gear before moving between lakes — it’s required by Michigan law and addressed in the Township’s Aquatic Nuisances Ordinance.

  • Never dump bait buckets or aquariums into any lake or stream, and don’t move firewood.
  • Report suspicious plants or animals to your lake association or the Benzie Conservation District — early detection is the best defense.

Where can I find water quality information for our lakes?

  • Annual water quality reports are posted on the Township website’s Water Quality Reports page.
  • Local monitoring results and lake health updates are available from the Crystal Lake Watershed Association, Platte Lake Association, Little Platte Lake Association, and Benzie Conservation District.
