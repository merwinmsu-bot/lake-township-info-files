Planning Commission

The Planning Commission reviews site plans, special land use requests, and the Township Master Plan, and is currently developing a comprehensive new Zoning Ordinance (Draft B1, October 2025) to replace the current ordinance. Key proposed changes include:

  • Stronger environmental protections for Crystal Lake, including a mandatory 35-foot shoreline vegetation buffer and impervious surface caps.
  • New provisions for accessory dwelling units (ADUs), solar energy, and short-term rentals.
  • A reorganized, three-tier permit approval system (Zoning Administrator / Site Plan Review / Special Use Permit).

Public hearings will be noticed on the Meetings & Agendas page and the Township Hall bulletin board. Contact the Zoning Administrator or attend a Planning Commission meeting to provide input.
