Welcome to Lake Township!

Your Short-Term Renter Guide — Benzie County, Northern Michigan, beautiful lake country.

Welcome, neighbor for a week! Lake Township is a residential community — not a resort zone. Please enjoy your stay and help us preserve the charm that brought you here in the first place.

Emergency — Call 911. Benzie County Sheriff (non-emergency): 231-882-4484 · Paul Oliver Memorial Hospital: 231-352-2200 · Lake Township: 231-325-5202

The Good Neighbor Rules

Quiet Hours: 10 PM – 8 AM

Keep noise considerate of neighbors at ALL hours — voices, music, and outdoor activities. Repeated complaints can result in fines and affect the property’s STR license.

Parking: Use Your Driveway

Use the off-street parking provided. On-street parking is prohibited. Check with your host for specific parking rules at this property.

Garbage & Recycling

Leave no trace! Garbage must be in proper covered containers. See your pickup day in the property details your host provided. Recycling drop-off: Homestead Township Hall (Honor) or 404 Parkview Lane, Frankfort (behind Stormcloud Brewery).

Fires & Fireworks

Open fire pits must be 25+ feet from the property line. Ask your host about fire devices. Fireworks are NOT permitted except a few dates — visit laketwp.org for more information. This is strictly enforced.

Pets

Pets must be leashed at all times outside — parks, streets, beaches, everywhere. Pick up and dispose of all pet waste. Pets disturbing neighbors violate township ordinances.

Occupancy Limits

Max occupancy = 2 per legal bedroom + 2 additional guests. Maximum occupants regardless of bedrooms is 12. Exceeding the limit is a violation of Lake Township ordinance.

Watercraft & Lake Rules

  • Towing Persons on any Device Hours: One hour before sunrise and one hour after sunset if towed behind a boat; 8:00 am to sunset if towed behind a personal water craft.
  • Gas Engine Restrictions: Some lakes in Lake Township prohibit gasoline-powered motors entirely. Always confirm with your host before launching a motorized vessel.
  • Life Jackets Required: All children under 6 must wear a Coast Guard-approved personal flotation device at all times on the water — Michigan state law.
  • No-Wake Zones & Swim Areas: Reduce speed and give wide berth to designated swim areas, anchored boats, docks, and marked no-wake buoys. Violators may be cited by the DNR.
  • Kayaks, Canoes & Non-Motorized Craft: Always yield to swimmers. Carry required safety equipment. Helmets recommended in moving water. The Betsie and Platte rivers are popular paddling routes nearby.
  • Boat Launches & Docks: Use public boat launches only unless your host has granted permission to use a private dock or launch. Leave ramps clear and respect the queue.
  • Fishing Licenses: A valid Michigan DNR fishing license is required for anyone 17 or older. Licenses available online at michigan.gov/dnr or at local sporting goods stores.

Leave It Better Than You Found It

  • Pack Out All Trash: Carry a bag on every beach or trail outing. Benzie County has no beachside garbage service — pack it in, pack it out.
  • Stay on Designated Trails: Dune vegetation, shoreline grasses, and wetland plants are fragile. Foot traffic off-trail causes erosion that can take decades to heal.
  • Respect Wildlife: Do not feed deer, birds, or any wildlife. Keep a safe distance from nesting shorebirds. Dogs must be leashed near dunes and natural areas to protect nesting species.
  • Recycling Drop-Off Locations: Homestead Township Hall (US 31, Honor), 404 Parkview Lane, Frankfort (behind Stormcloud Brewery), and Village of Beulah library parking lot.
  • Report Property Damage Promptly: Notify your host immediately of any damage to the property or adjacent land.
  • Leave Natural Items in Place: Do not remove driftwood, rocks, shells, wildflowers, plants, or any natural material from beaches, dunes, or trails.
  • Report Safety or Environmental Concerns: Contact your Local Contact Person for property issues. For environmental hazards (spills, erosion, illegal dumping), MDNR: 231-775-9727. Public safety (non-emergency): Sheriff’s Office 231-882-4484.

Explore the Area — Things to Do Near Lake Township

National & State Parks & Natural Areas

  • Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore — Towering sand dunes, pristine Lake Michigan beaches, the Dune Climb, and Pierce Stocking Scenic Drive. nps.gov/slbe · 231-326-5134
  • Point Betsie Lighthouse — A beloved and historic lighthouse right here in Lake Township on Lake Michigan, fully restored and open for tours in season.
  • Lake Township Park — A scenic, multi-use recreation site at the mouth of the Platte River where it meets Lake Michigan. Excellent swimming, picnicking, and views.
  • Platte River & Crystal River Paddling — Gin-clear spring-fed rivers perfect for tubing and kayaking. Rentals available locally.

Arts, Culture & History

  • Interlochen Center for the Arts — World-renowned performing arts campus with public concerts all summer long. interlochen.org · 231-276-7200
  • Benzie Area Historical Museum — Explore the logging, maritime, and rural heritage of Benzie County. Free admission, located in Benzonia. benziemuseum.org
  • Elizabeth Lane Oliver Center for the Arts — A premier nonprofit gallery in a historic, renovated 1934 Coast Guard station in Frankfort. oliverartcenterfrankfort.org · 231-352-4151
  • Frankfort & Elberta Waterfront — Stroll the piers of Frankfort Harbor and the Elberta shoreline for stunning Lake Michigan sunsets, public beaches, and charming downtown shops and dining.
Thank you for visiting Lake Township and Benzie County. Your respect for our community helps ensure that this beautiful corner of Michigan stays exactly that — beautiful. We hope to see you back next season!
