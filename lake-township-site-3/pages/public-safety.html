Public Safety

Fire protection, emergency medical services, and law enforcement for Lake Township.

In any emergency, dial 911. All fire, medical, and police calls are handled through Benzie County Central Dispatch.

Fire Protection

Lake Township contracts with the City of Frankfort to provide fire protection services. These services are funded through a voter-approved fire millage paid by Lake Township property owners.

In addition to the firefighting equipment housed at the Frankfort Fire Barn, Lake Township maintains a fire truck at the Township Hall fire barn.

The Frankfort Fire Department engine that protects Lake Township

Emergency Medical Services (EMS)

Benzie County EMS is the Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulance service provider for Benzie County, provided through the City of Frankfort Fire Department’s service area covering Lake Township. The service is funded through a voter-approved property tax millage paid by county residents.

Benzie County EMS provides Advanced Life Support coverage 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Three staffed ambulances are in service during daytime hours, while two staffed ambulances provide overnight coverage. During the summer months, three staffed ambulances are in service around the clock to meet increased seasonal demand.

Sheriff’s Office

Law enforcement services for Lake Township are provided by the Benzie County Sheriff’s Office.
