2 — Site Plan / Drawing Requirements

Two copies of a dimensioned site plan or survey of the property must be submitted. Plans for commercial projects must be signed and sealed by a licensed professional.

3 — Floor Plans & Structural Drawings

Attach dimensioned, scaled floor plan(s) or layout(s) of all existing and proposed structures. Sealed engineered plans must be attached for commercial projects.

4 — Zoning Ordinance Compliance Statement

Attach a written statement showing compliance with the standards set forth in the Lake Township Zoning Ordinance, including, but not limited to: Article IV (Environmental Provisions), Article V (Site Development Standards), and Article XII (Special Land Uses).
