Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)

Township records are available to the public under the Michigan Freedom of Information Act. Submit a written request describing the records you’re seeking to the Township’s FOIA Coordinator (the Township Clerk) by mail, email, or in person. The Township must respond within 5 business days (extendable by 10). Fees may apply for search, copying, and mailing costs as allowed by law.
