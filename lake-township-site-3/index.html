About this site

A local copy of the forms you need

This site was built so Lake Township residents and property owners can keep permit applications, fee schedules, and resident guides on hand even without a network connection — useful at camp, on the water, or anywhere the signal doesn’t reach. Fill out a form in your browser, print it or save it as a PDF, sign it, and bring or mail it to the Township Office.

Forms here mirror the Township’s official paper applications. When in doubt, or for the very latest versions, contact the Township Office directly.
