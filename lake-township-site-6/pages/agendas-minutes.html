Meetings & Agendas

Board and Planning Commission meeting schedules, agendas, and minutes.

Regular Meeting Schedule

  • Township Board: First Thursday of each month, 7:00 PM, Township Hall.
  • Planning Commission: Dates posted on this page and the Township Hall bulletin board once finalized.
  • Board of Review: Meets each March — specific dates posted in advance.

Special meetings, joint hearings, and public hearing notices are also posted on the Township Hall bulletin board.

Content coming soon

Posted agendas & minutes archive

Digital agendas and approved meeting minutes are being organized for online posting and will appear here.

The Township is planning an agendas-and-minutes archive modeled on Benzie County’s Board of Commissioners page — searchable by date, with agenda packets and approved minutes posted together. Once that system is live, this page will link directly to it.

In the meantime

Call the Township Office to confirm the next meeting date and to request current agendas or past minutes.
