How forms work: Each form below is a digital copy of the Township’s official paper application. Fill it in, click Print / Save as PDF in the toolbar, sign the printed copy, and deliver or mail it to the Township Office. Your entries are saved on this computer only (never sent anywhere) so you can safely close the tab and pick up where you left off.

Not sure which form you need?

Building a shed, deck, addition, or new structure? Start with the Building Project Best Practices guide — it walks through permits in the right order, starting with the Land Use Permit.

Requesting a variance or appealing a zoning decision? Read the Zoning Board of Appeals guide first to understand the practical difficulty standard.

Still unsure? Call the Township Office at 231-325-5202 and staff can point you to the right application.

Requesting Township public records? See the How Do I? — Documents & Downloads section, then use the FOIA Request form above.
