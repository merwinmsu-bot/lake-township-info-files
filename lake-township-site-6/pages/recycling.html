Recycling & Material Management

Lake Township does not provide curbside trash collection — here’s how residents handle waste, bulky items, and recycling.

Benzie County Recycling Program

Benzie County operates free 24/7 recycling drop-off sites throughout the county. Curbside recycling pick-up is not available. See the Benzie County Materials Management page for current drop-off locations, hours, and accepted materials: benzieco.gov/government/refuse___recycling.php.

Questions about recycling? Call the Benzie County Recycling Coordinator at 231-882-0554.

Township Clean-Up Days

The township hosts free Clean-Up Days each year for residents to dispose of bulky items. Clean-Up Days are typically held on the second Saturdays of June and August, 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM (or until trucks are full) — dates vary each year, so watch the Township home page for current-year details.

The next Clean-Up Day is Saturday, August 8, 2026, from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM at the Lake Township Hall (M-22 & Lake Michigan Road, south driveway entrance).

Not accepted at Clean-Up Day: automotive batteries and tires, chemicals or hazardous waste, motor oil, paint and thinners, yard waste, concrete, refrigerators containing Freon, and lumber over 4 feet. No commercial or contractor dumping.

Private Trash Collection Vendors

Lake Township does not provide curbside trash collection. Residents arrange their own service directly with a private hauler. There are a few haulers currently operating in Benzie County — contact them for pricing and to confirm they serve your location.
