Section 9 — Owner Affidavit and Authorization

I agree that the statements made above are true, and if found not to be true this application and any approval will be void. I agree to comply with the conditions and regulations provided with this parcel division, and to give permission for officials of Lake Township, Benzie County, and the State of Michigan to enter the property for purposes of inspection. I understand this is only a parcel division under the applicable local land division ordinance, the local zoning ordinance, and the State Land Division Act (MCL 560.101 et seq.), and does not include any representation or conveyance of rights in any other statute, building code, zoning ordinance, deed restriction, or other property rights. I understand that per MCL 560.267, any sale of lands subdivided or otherwise partitioned in violation of this act is voidable at the option of the purchaser. I understand that if this land division is approved, I am required to file one deed per new parcel legal description (including the remaining original parcel) within 90 days of the approval. Failure to timely file required deeds will result in the approval being voided and will require a new application and fees.
