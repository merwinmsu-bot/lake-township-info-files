Land Use Permit & Fee Schedule · Effective March 7, 2013

Fee Schedule

Current fees for land use permits, zoning hearings, site plan review, and land divisions.

It is the policy of Lake Township that fees levied for various permits and services shall bear a reasonable relationship with actual cost to the Township. Fees collected shall be used to offset the actual costs of administering the Lake Township Zoning Ordinance, including postage, photocopying, newspaper public hearing advertisements, notice to adjoining property owners, per diem, salaries, wages of staff and officers, mileage, phone and fax charges, maps, reproductions, inspections, and other staff review and preparation time.

Fees include publication of the legal notice, postage to mail notices to adjacent property owners, per diem, wages, preparation time, copy costs, mileage, map reproductions, and inspections. Any cost incurred in excess of the above fees will be billed to the petitioner, subject to Additional Costs of Review. A financial report will be supplied upon request.

Additional Costs of Review: An applicant shall be required to pay such fees (in addition to the above) in the amount determined by “actual costs,” as documented by Lake Township and billed as incurred — such as attorney fees, professional planner, engineer, surveyor, architect, and landscape architect hired by Lake Township to review submitted plans and applications. A Land Use Permit will not be issued until these fees are paid in full by the applicant.

Land Use Permits

  • Failure to obtain a Land Use Permit before starting a project will result in double the permit fee.
  • Failure to notify the Township of changes to submitted plans will incur an additional fee of one half the original fee.
  • Renewal of an expired Land Use Permit will be one half the original fee.
