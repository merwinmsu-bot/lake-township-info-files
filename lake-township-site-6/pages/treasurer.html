Treasurer

Property tax collection and payments for Lake Township.

Tax Due Dates & Interest

Lake Township summer tax bills are mailed July 1 and payment is due by September 14. By law, interest will be assessed on taxes not paid by the due date. As of September 15, interest accrues at a rate of 1% of the unpaid balance for each calendar month thereafter (1% added September 15–30, another 1% October 1, November 1, etc.).

Winter tax bills are mailed December 1 each year and are due February 14. After that date, ALL unpaid taxes (summer & winter) are assessed a 3% penalty. Lake Township continues to collect those taxes until the last day of February each year. After that date, property taxes are considered delinquent and are turned over to the county treasurer for collection.

Summer Tax Deferment

Payment of summer taxes may be deferred without penalty or interest until the day winter taxes are due in February, for the principal residence of any taxpayer who meets the following criteria:

  • A senior citizen 62 years or older; OR
  • Paraplegic or quadriplegic; OR
  • Totally and permanently disabled; OR
  • An eligible serviceperson (or widow/widower); OR
  • An eligible veteran (or widow/widower); OR
  • Blind.

In addition to meeting one of the criteria above, the household income must not exceed $40,000 per year.

Online Payments

Since 2019, Lake Township has offered an online payment option. You may pay your property taxes by credit card (fees apply, varying percentage rates) or by ACH check (flat rate of $2 per transaction). Simply go to the BS&A Online payment portal; you will need your parcel number and amount due.

You may pay for more than one parcel per transaction — just add that information in the notes section. This service is provided through a third party; the Township does not make or pay any money for this service.

Returned payments: All forms of payment must clear banking institutions or the payment is void and taxes are considered unpaid. All returned checks (paper and ACH) will incur a $25 returned check fee, payable to Lake Township.

A Note on Postmarks & Post-Dated Checks

Please DO NOT send post-dated checks. Our bank will not honor them, and according to the Michigan Uniform Commercial Code, “checks are drafts, payable on demand, and drawn on a bank.” Since a post-dated check is not payable on demand, we cannot accept it as payment for taxes and are required to return it to the issuer with notification that their taxes are not paid.

We do accept legible postmark dates for payments close to the due dates and/or end of year.

Copies & Receipts

After the July 1 and December 1 mailing dates, we can e-mail you a copy of your tax bill, or send additional copies anywhere you request — just contact the Treasurer’s Office.

Lake Township will always mail you a receipt after each payment — no need for a special request or a self-addressed envelope.
