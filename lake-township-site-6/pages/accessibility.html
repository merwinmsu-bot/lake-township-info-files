Accessibility Statement

Lake Township’s commitment to an accessible website.

Lake Township is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for residents and visitors of all abilities. We aim to comply with applicable standards, including the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), and we continue to improve the usability of this site for everyone.
